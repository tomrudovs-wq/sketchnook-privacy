Questions, problems or ideas: write to tomrudovs@gmail.com. We answer every message, usually within two days.

Restoring books you bought

Open Settings in Sketchnook (the gear on Home) and tap Restore purchases. Books are restored from the account that bought them: your Apple Account on iPhone and iPad, your Google account on Android. A set restores the books you chose for it.

Where your artwork is saved

On iPhone and iPad, Save puts the picture in the Files app, in On My iPhone or On My iPad > Sketchnook. On Android it goes to Downloads > Sketchnook. Share sends a picture or a short drawing video to any app you choose.

Your drawings and progress

Everything you draw stays on your device. There is no account, so a new device starts fresh, except for the books you bought, which you can restore.
